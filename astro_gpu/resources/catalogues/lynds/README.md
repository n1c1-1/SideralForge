# Lynds catalogue of dark nebulae (VizieR VII/7A)

- File: `vii7a.tsv.gz`, VizieR's tab-separated answer for table `VII/7A/ldn`, every column plus `_RAJ2000` /
  `_DEJ2000` (J2000, degrees, computed by VizieR from the B1950 positions), unchanged, gzip-compressed. 1802 nebulae.
- Origin: CDS VizieR catalogue VII/7A, served by the VizieR mirror at IDIA, Cape Town (vizier.idia.ac.za); Strasbourg
  and the Harvard mirror did not answer on the day. Query:
  `https://vizier.idia.ac.za/viz-bin/asu-tsv?-source=VII/7A/ldn&-out.all&-out.add=_RAJ2000,_DEJ2000&-oc.form=d&-out.max=unlimited`
- Version: VII/7A as served on 2 November 2026 (the date is in the file's header).
- Made with: `tools/catalogues/build_objects.py`.
- Terms: the CDS conditions of use (https://cds.unistra.fr/vizier-org/licences_vizier.html): free for research and
  education, with the acknowledgement below.
- Used for: finding a dark nebula by its name ("LDN 1622") in the target search. Not drawn on the images.

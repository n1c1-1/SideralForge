# Glossary of the GUI

English is the language of the GUI; the French column is the French translation (Settings > Language).
Rules: short names a Siril or PixInsight user recognises at once; one name per object; no home-made jargon; a neutral
tone (no "you/your" in labels, no "tu" in French); numbers with their unit.
